The Idea

Every coffee drinker has experienced it.

The first sip tastes like milk.
The last sip tastes like pure sugar.

After seeing it happen again and again, I realized it wasn't just a bad day—it was a pattern. Instead of complaining, I built a way to measure it.

Why This Exists

Traditional review sites tell you whether a coffee shop is popular. Stir My Coffee tells you whether they consistently prepare your drink correctly.

This platform exists to celebrate locations that consistently deliver quality while giving customers real-world data instead of random opinions.

How It Works

  • Find a coffee shop near you.
  • Rate whether your drink was prepared correctly.
  • Help build community-powered performance data.
  • Discover which locations consistently get it right.

Every vote helps build a clearer picture of real-world consistency.

About the Creator

Builder. Operator. Problem Solver.

I'm Jon Scott, a local IT professional, founder of HomeNet Hudson, web developer at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), and a transfer student at Quinsigamond Community College preparing to attend SANS University for cybersecurity.

Stir My Coffee wasn't built by a startup team or backed by investors.

It was built one feature at a time between client IT work, college classes, restaurant management, late nights, and early mornings.

Before working in technology, I spent years leading professional kitchens and was recognized as one of Thumbtack's Top 10 chefs in Boston.

Kitchens teach one lesson quickly: Consistency matters.

That same philosophy now drives how I build software.

When I noticed the same coffee preparation mistakes happening over and over, I didn't see an isolated problem.

I saw a broken process.

So I built a system to measure it.

Built From Scratch

Stir My Coffee was designed and developed independently using original code. It wasn't assembled from website builders or plug-ins.

  • Nationwide coffee shop database
  • Custom ranking engine
  • Interactive mapping
  • Community voting system
  • Mobile-first web application
  • Google Play app

Why It Matters

This isn't really about coffee.

It's about standards.

When the same detail gets skipped thousands of times every day, it stops being a mistake and becomes a measurable pattern.

Stir My Coffee exists to recognize the locations that consistently get it right.
